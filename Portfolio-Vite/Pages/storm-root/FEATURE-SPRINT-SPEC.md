# Feature Sprint Spec

## All Feature Packets

### Pieces

		- Sprint objective: Establish a solid foundation for piece manipulation and interaction
		- Player promise: Understand and effectively use pieces to gain advantages in gameplay
		- Branch reason: Pieces form the basis of gameplay and are crucial to player progression

		#### Branch Slices
		- Piece Movement
  - Implement basic piece movement controls
  - Add piece rotation functionality
  - Create piece queueing system

		#### Checkpoints

- Basic piece movement functioning
- Piece rotation working
- Piece queueing system integrated

		#### Acceptance Signals

- Player can move pieces around the game world
- Piece rotation is smooth and responsive
- Piece queueing allows for strategic placement

		#### Scope

- Focus on 2D piece movement for now
- Do not add any piece-specific abilities yet

		#### Risks

- Piece movement controls may feel clunky
- Players may struggle to understand piece rotation

### Ember

		- Sprint objective: Integrate ember abilities and interactions into the gameplay loop
		- Player promise: Use ember abilities to enhance piece manipulation and overcome obstacles
		- Branch reason: Ember abilities provide unique gameplay mechanics and tactical options

		#### Branch Slices
		- Ember Abilities
  - Design basic ember abilities
  - Integrate abilities into gameplay
  - Add ability cooldowns

		#### Checkpoints

- First ember ability functioning
- All basic abilities integrated
- Cooldown system working

		#### Acceptance Signals

- Player can use ember abilities to manipulate pieces
- Abilities provide clear benefits to gameplay
- Cooldown system is balanced

		#### Scope

- Limit initial abilities to simple enhancements
- Do not integrate ember abilities with pieces yet

		#### Risks

- Ember abilities may feel underpowered or overpowered
- Players may struggle to understand ability cooldowns

### Crash

		- Sprint objective: Create a crash system that challenges players and rewards skill
		- Player promise: Master the crash system to gain advantages and unlock new content
		- Branch reason: The crash system adds depth and complexity to the gameplay loop

		#### Branch Slices
		- Crash Mechanics
  - Design crash system
  - Integrate crash system into gameplay
  - Add crash-specific rewards

		#### Checkpoints

- Crash system functioning
- Crash rewards integrated
- Players can complete crashes

		#### Acceptance Signals

- Crash system adds challenge and excitement to gameplay
- Crash rewards provide clear benefits
- Players can complete crashes with some difficulty

		#### Scope

- Limit initial crash rewards to basic unlocks
- Do not integrate crash system with pieces or ember abilities yet

		#### Risks

- Crash system may feel too punishing or too forgiving
- Players may struggle to understand crash rewards
