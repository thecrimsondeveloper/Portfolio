# Build Spec

## Implementation Chapters

- Chapter 1: Foundation - Establish the core mechanics of piece manipulation and interaction, and introduce the player to the subterranean environment. Create a low-risk route for players to learn the basics of movement and piece interaction.
- Chapter 2: Exploration - Layer in ember interactions and mixed enemy waves to challenge the player and introduce guard reads. Use the exploration loops to teach players about piece and ember interactions, and how to use them to survive and reposition.
- Chapter 3: Mastery - Force mastery through stacked hazards, elite guard encounters, and a final unify choice. Use rewards such as unify, evolve, discover, and expand to motivate players and keep them engaged.
- Chapter 4: Polishing - Refine the gameplay experience, balance the difficulty, and fix any bugs or issues that arise during development. Use player feedback to make any necessary adjustments and improvements.
- Chapter 5: Launch - Release the game to the public and market it to target audiences. Use post-launch support to address any issues or bugs that arise, and consider adding additional content or features based on player feedback.
