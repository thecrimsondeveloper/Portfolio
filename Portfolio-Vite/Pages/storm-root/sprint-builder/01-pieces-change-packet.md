# Pieces Change Packet

			## Sprint Objective

			Establish a solid foundation for piece manipulation and interaction

			## Player Promise

			Understand and effectively use pieces to gain advantages in gameplay

			## Why This Branch Exists

			Pieces form the basis of gameplay and are crucial to player progression

			## Detail Fractalization

			- Piece Movement
  - Implement basic piece movement controls
  - Add piece rotation functionality
  - Create piece queueing system

			## Simulated Checkpoints


- Basic piece movement functioning
- Piece rotation working
- Piece queueing system integrated

			## Acceptance Signals


- Player can move pieces around the game world
- Piece rotation is smooth and responsive
- Piece queueing allows for strategic placement

			## Change Packet Scope


- Focus on 2D piece movement for now
- Do not add any piece-specific abilities yet

			## Risks And Unknowns


- Piece movement controls may feel clunky
- Players may struggle to understand piece rotation

			## Implementation Notes

			- Keep this packet isolated to the current game folder.
			- Use the checkpoints as simulated gates before full implementation.
			- Treat each branch slice as a sub-sprint, not as a merged refactor.
