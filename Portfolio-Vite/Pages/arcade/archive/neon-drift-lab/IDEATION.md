    ### Expansion Ideas
    Seed Details:
A survival game set on a procedurally generated alien planet with distinct biomes, where players must gather resources, craft tools, and build shelters to survive and eventually escape.

Expansion Ideas:

1. **Advanced Crafting System**: Introduce an advanced crafting system that allows players to create more complex items and structures. This could include new tools, weapons, and machinery that can help players exploit the planet's resources more efficiently.
2. **Environmental Hazards**: Add new environmental hazards, such as extreme weather events, toxic gases, and deadly creatures. Players would need to adapt to these challenges by building appropriate shelters, creating new tools, and developing new strategies.
3. **Faction System**: Implement a faction system that allows players to join or create factions, each with their own goals, values, and rewards. This could lead to new alliances, rivalries, and opportunities for trade and cooperation.
4. **Space Exploration**: Allow players to build and pilot their own spacecraft, enabling them to explore other planets and systems. This could lead to new resources, challenges, and storylines.
5. **Multiplayer Mode**: Introduce a multiplayer mode that allows players to collaborate or compete with each other. This could include cooperative building projects, player-versus-player combat, and trading systems.
6. **Modding Support**: Provide modding support to allow players to create and share their own custom content, such as new biomes, items, and enemies.
7. **Research and Technology System**: Implement a research and technology system that allows players to unlock new technologies and abilities as they progress. This could include advanced building materials, renewable energy sources, and new methods of transportation.
8. **Dynamic Storyline**: Add a dynamic storyline that changes based on player choices and actions. This could include branching narrative paths, multiple endings, and emergent storytelling.
9. **Survival of the Fittest**: Introduce a "survival of the fittest" mechanic where players compete against each other for resources and territory. This could lead to new strategies, alliances, and conflicts.
10. **Underground Civilization**: Discover an underground civilization and interact with its inhabitants. This could lead to new quests, storylines, and rewards.
