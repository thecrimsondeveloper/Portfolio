
    # ACTION EXTRACTION

    **Action Map (Neon Drift Lab)**

1. Stealthily navigate the flooded market district while avoiding mischievous spirits.
2. Collect scattered pieces of the ancient artifact as you progress through the game.
3. Assemble the map and reveal the hidden layout of the district.
4. Activate the ritual to appease the spirits and protect the neighborhood.
5. Gather story fragments and artifact sets to uncover the neighborhood's history.
6. Utilize inventory-lite object use to solve puzzles and progress.

**Detail Items (Neon Drift Lab Prototype)**

1. Flooded market district environment with hand-painted textures.
2. Stealth crouch and rhythm-based stepping movement mechanics.
3. Ancient artifact collection and assembly mechanics.
4. Mischievous spirits as antagonists with unique behaviors.
5. Story fragments and artifact sets as rewards.
6. Inventory-lite object use for puzzle-solving.
