# Atlas Archive DNA

## Theme
- Catalogues, maps, and the logic of routes.
- An archive that organizes paths as if they were living records.
- A quiet, deliberate focus on choice and consequence.

## Movement DNA
- The player moves through corridors of maps and decision points.
- Movement is about selecting routes and following patterns.
- The experience is tactical and reflective, not frenetic.

## Story Modules
- Atlas Hall: enter the hall of routes and prepare your choice.
- Shattered Mirrors: explore mirrored paths and alternate outcomes.
- Ancient Roots: follow the living archive toward the final confrontation.
- Rift Path: commit to the opening that leads to the saga.
- Atlas Study: learn the archive's rules before the last step.
