# Obsidian Echoes DNA

## Theme
- Mystic ruins and forgotten archives.
- Slow, exploratory movement through tight passageways.
- Discovery through sound, shadow, and tactile interaction.

## Movement DNA
- Navigation is based on listening to echoes and choosing paths.
- Movement feels deliberate and grounded, with a focus on memory-led exploration.
- The environment responds to your touch and the rhythm of your progress.

## Story Modules
- Ruin Gate: enter the archive and awaken the echoes.
- Whispering Corridor: move through glyph-lit passageways.
- Fractured Map: shift routes and choose your direction.
- Resonance Shrine: use rhythm to unlock the hidden story.
- Shadow Passage: accept the challenge and continue the journey.
