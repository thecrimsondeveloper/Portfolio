Hidden Relay Run is a delivery puzzle experience where the player navigates an overgrown, flooded market at festival night, routing packages through animated relay gates and plant corridors.
The core gameplay blends timing, route planning, and environmental interaction: players must use swinging movement, precision jumps, and dynamic gate timing to reach delivery nodes.
Players collect power modules to upgrade movement and unlock alternate paths, while rescue and repair objectives reward exploration and completion loops.
