# Clockwork Chronicle DNA

## Theme
- Mechanical movement and timeworn machinery.
- Workshop, tracks, and archive aesthetics.
- A structured, engineered world of cogs, rails, and memory reels.

## Movement DNA
- Motion is driven by clockwork rhythm and track choice.
- Players feel the push and pull of train-like momentum.
- Choices are made by selecting reels and routes in an archive hub.

## Story Modules
- Arrival Platform: enter the chronicle and feel the movement of tracks.
- Captain's Log: inspect the memories that reveal a mechanical betrayal.
- Archive Reel: choose which history reel to engage.
- Hidden Manifest: discover the names and routes that shape the next passage.
- Clockwork Corridor: activate the mechanism that sends you onward.
