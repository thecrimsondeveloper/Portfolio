# Spectral Passage DNA

## Theme
- Memory currents and ritual motion.
- A journey through intangible streams and ancestral patterns.
- Light, lanterns, and flowing narrative energy.

## Movement DNA
- Movement mimics gliding through currents rather than walking.
- Choices are grounded in selecting memory threads and balancing momentum.
- The experience is about holding one strand while letting others fade.

## Story Modules
- Memory Gateway: step into the current and begin the passage.
- Memory Current: choose the thread of warrior or archivist memory.
- Guiding Lantern: follow the light toward the next archive.
- Atlas Path: continue along a hidden route to the final archive.
- Anchored Realm: bind the passage's energy before the finale.
