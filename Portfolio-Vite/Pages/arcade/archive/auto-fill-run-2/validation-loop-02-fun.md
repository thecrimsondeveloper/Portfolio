        **Things needed to make this game fun:**

- 1. Engaging and immersive storyline: A compelling narrative can draw players in and make them care about the world and characters they are interacting with.
- 2. Intuitive and responsive controls: Players should feel in control of their actions and have a smooth, satisfying gameplay experience.
- 3. Dynamic and challenging gameplay: The game should provide a sense of challenge and reward, with a variety of objectives and enemies to keep players engaged.
- 4. Diverse and interesting characters: Characters with unique personalities, backstories, and motivations can make the game more engaging and relatable.
- 5. Rich and detailed environments: A visually appealing and immersive game world can enhance the player's experience and make the game more engaging.
- 6. Variety of weapons and abilities: Giving players a range of tools and abilities to use can keep the gameplay fresh and allow for different playstyles.
- 7. Co-operative and/or competitive multiplayer: Playing with others can add a new level of fun and replayability to the game.
- 8. Regular updates and new content: Keeping the game fresh with new challenges, events, and rewards can help to keep players engaged and coming back for more.
- 9. Balanced difficulty: The game should be challenging, but not so difficult that it becomes frustrating or overwhelming for players.
- 10. Achievable goals and sense of progression: Players should feel like they are making progress and achieving something as they play, whether it's unlocking new abilities, leveling up, or completing challenges.
