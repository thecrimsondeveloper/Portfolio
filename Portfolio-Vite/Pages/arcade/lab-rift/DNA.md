# Riftbound Saga DNA

## Theme
- Final convergence of storylines and narrative energy.
- A mystical rift rather than cosmic space.
- Legacy, closure, and the shape of endings.

## Movement DNA
- Movement is dramatic and decisive, focused on the final choice.
- The player moves through tension and release rather than physical speed.
- Choices are weighted by the whole journey that came before.

## Story Modules
- Rift Portal: arrive at the tear and feel the convergence.
- Rewritten Saga: choose a new ending for the narrative.
- Sealed Rift: bind the story with memory and legacy.
- Resolution: let the journey close with all threads resolved.
