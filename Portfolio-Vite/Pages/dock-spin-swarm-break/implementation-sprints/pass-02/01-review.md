# Sprint Review

		## Thoughts

		With movement chosen as the first packet, the best second slice is a single enemy archetype supported by one route hazard. That keeps scope narrow while making the frozen dock premise feel more specific: drones should cut lanes and punish hesitation near bridge choke points.

		## Target Changes


- Turn one enemy behavior into a lane-blocking drone that sweeps bridge paths instead of just chasing.
- Make one hazard read like a dock bridge breaker or frozen surge field rather than a generic damage circle.
- Update status and objective language so the player understands lane denial and extraction pressure together.

		## Risks


- Adding too much enemy logic could destabilize the readable first-run flow.
- A stronger blocker pattern could feel unfair if spin bursts do not still create escape windows.
- Hazard dressing could become cosmetic if it is not reflected in gameplay messaging.

		## Acceptance Signals


- At least one enemy clearly reads as a route-blocking drone rather than a generic pursuer.
- One obstacle on the route is visibly tied to dock infrastructure or winter surge pressure.
- The player can explain the hazard-and-enemy combo after a single run.

		## Target Files Or Surfaces


- game.js
- story-structure.json
- index.html
